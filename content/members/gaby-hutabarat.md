Gaby Hutabarat is a student in the Electrical Engineering department at CU Boulder and is participating in the Summer Program for Undergraduate Research. She is currently working on further improving the documentation of the Open Radar Code Architecture (ORCA) and researching the capabilities of bistatic and monostatic radars to investigate the vertical water distribution in tree canopies. Gaby’s interests outside of schoolwork include crocheting, puzzles, and listening to music.
